A computerised **Brother embroidery machine** for turning digital designs into
precisely stitched patterns — including with **conductive threads**, enabling
functional electronics to be embroidered directly into fabric.

**What it's used for**

- Prototyping **e-textile** sensors, electrodes, and interconnects with
  conductive yarns.
- Integrating **haptic actuators** and wiring into garments and soft, wearable
  form factors — a natural extension of ACUTE's vibrotactile research toward
  everyday-wearable devices.
- Rapid iteration of wearable and soft-hardware designs, from pattern to stitched
  prototype.

**Access for collaborators**

Available to researchers, designers, and artists exploring smart textiles and
wearable haptics. We can support conductive-thread selection, digitising designs,
and integrating electronics.
