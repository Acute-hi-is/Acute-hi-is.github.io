A **Brüel & Kjær Type 4206-T** impedance tube kit for characterising how
materials interact with sound. It supports two standard measurements from the
same bench:

**Sound absorption** (two-microphone transfer-function method, ISO 10534-2 /
ASTM E1050)

- Measures a sample's **absorption coefficient**, **reflection coefficient**, and
  **normal acoustic impedance**.
- Two tube sizes cover a wide band: the **large tube (100 mm)** for ~50 Hz–1.6 kHz
  and the **small tube (29 mm)** for ~500 Hz–6.4 kHz.
- A sliding sample-holder piston allows an adjustable air gap behind the sample.

**Transmission loss** (four-microphone transfer-matrix method, ASTM E2611)

- Measures how much sound a material **blocks** as it passes through, using the
  4-microphone source-and-receiving-tube configuration.
- Useful for evaluating panels, barriers, and layered/absorptive materials.

Measurements are acquired with the lab's **Brüel & Kjær LAN-XI** front end and an
open-source analysis toolchain, so results are reproducible and licence-free.

**Access for collaborators**

Available for acoustic material testing — bring your samples and we'll help with
sample preparation, calibration, measurement, and analysis.
