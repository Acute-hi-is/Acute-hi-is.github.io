The lab's audio backbone is built around an **RME MADIface XT** interface paired
with **multichannel AD/DA converters**, giving a large, low-latency, tightly
synchronised set of analogue inputs and outputs over MADI.

**What it's used for**

- Driving **loudspeaker arrays** and **spatial-audio rigs** with many
  synchronised output channels.
- Capturing **microphone arrays** and multichannel experiments with matched,
  low-latency inputs.
- Playback and recording for psychoacoustic experiments, auralisation, and
  binaural/immersive audio work — including with the KEMAR manikin in the
  anechoic chamber.

**Access for collaborators**

Available as the signal backbone for multichannel measurement, playback, and
recording setups. We can help integrate it with your transducers, arrays, and
experiment software.
