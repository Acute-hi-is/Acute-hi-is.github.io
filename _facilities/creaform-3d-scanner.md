A handheld **CREAFORM 3D scanner** for fast, metrology-grade capture of surfaces
and objects, producing accurate 3D meshes suitable for measurement, modelling,
and fabrication.

**What it's used for**

- Scanning **ears and pinnae** for HRTF research and the creation of custom and
  synthetic **pinna replicas**.
- Digitising anatomy and objects for **reverse engineering**, custom fixtures,
  and 3D-printable parts.
- Capturing accurate geometry to feed simulation, design, and manufacturing
  workflows.

**Access for collaborators**

Available for 3D capture and digitisation projects. We can advise on scanning
strategy, post-processing, and downstream mesh/CAD workflows.
