ACUTE operates a fully built **anechoic chamber** — a room whose walls, floor,
and ceiling are lined with absorptive wedges to suppress reflections, creating a
free-field environment with no audible echo down to a low cut-off frequency.

**What it's used for**

- Free-field characterisation of loudspeakers, actuators, and other transducers.
- Binaural and spatial-audio capture without room colouration (e.g. HRTF
  measurement together with the KEMAR manikin).
- Controlled psychoacoustic and perception experiments where reflections and
  background noise would otherwise confound results.
- Quiet measurement of low-level signals and directivity patterns.

**Access for collaborators**

The chamber is available to visiting researchers, industry partners, and artists
who need a reflection-free acoustic environment. We can support experiment
design, instrumentation, and integration with the lab's measurement chain.
