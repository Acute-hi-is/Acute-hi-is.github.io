The lab's **GRAS KEMAR** (Knowles Electronics Manikin for Acoustic Research) is a
head-and-torso simulator whose dimensions and ear geometry represent a median
adult, giving realistic, repeatable measurements of sound as it arrives at a
listener's ears.

**What it's used for**

- Measuring **head-related transfer functions (HRTFs)** and binaural cues — a
  core thread of ACUTE's spatial-audio work (see the Viking HRTF Dataset).
- Binaural recording for immersive audio, VR/AR, and listening tests.
- Evaluation of headphones, hearing aids, hearing protection, and other
  on/over-ear devices.
- Studies with custom and 3D-printed **pinna replicas** to isolate how ear shape
  shapes spatial hearing.

**Access for collaborators**

Available for binaural measurement campaigns and device evaluation. It pairs
directly with the anechoic chamber and the lab's multichannel audio front end.

See the manufacturer page for the base platform:
[GRAS KEMAR](https://www.grasacoustics.com/industries/audiology/kemar).
