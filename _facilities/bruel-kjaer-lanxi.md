A professional **Brüel & Kjær LAN-XI** sound-and-vibration acquisition front end,
supporting CCLD/IEPE transducers (microphones and accelerometers) and
externally-polarised measurement microphones, with a built-in signal generator
and a bandwidth of DC–25.6 kHz.

**What it's used for**

- Precision **sound & vibration measurement**: microphone and accelerometer
  acquisition, frequency response, and transfer-function work.
- Generator-driven excitation for loudspeaker and system characterisation.
- The acquisition backbone for the lab's **impedance-tube** measurements
  (absorption and transmission loss — see that facility).

The lab also maintains an **open-source Python toolchain** for the LAN-XI Open
API, so acquisition and analysis run without proprietary licences — useful for
reproducible, scriptable measurement campaigns.

**Access for collaborators**

Available for sound and vibration measurement projects, with support for
instrumentation, calibration, and analysis.
