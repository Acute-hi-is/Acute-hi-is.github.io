A professional **Brüel & Kjær LAN-XI** sound-and-vibration acquisition front end,
supporting CCLD/IEPE transducers (microphones and accelerometers) and
externally-polarised measurement microphones, with a built-in signal generator
and a bandwidth of DC–25.6 kHz.

**What it's used for**

- **Sound-absorption and acoustic material testing** with a **B&K Type 4206
  impedance tube** using the two-microphone transfer-function method
  (ISO 10534-2 / ASTM E1050) — measuring the absorption coefficient and surface
  impedance of material samples.
- General precision **sound & vibration measurement**: microphone and
  accelerometer acquisition, frequency response, and transfer-function work.
- Generator-driven excitation for loudspeaker and system characterisation.

The lab also maintains an **open-source Python toolchain** for the LAN-XI Open
API, so acquisition and analysis (including impedance-tube absorption) run
without proprietary licences — useful for reproducible, scriptable measurement
campaigns.

**Access for collaborators**

Available for material-absorption testing and sound/vibration measurement
projects, with support for measurement setup, calibration, and analysis.
