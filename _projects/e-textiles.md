Integrating textile-based sensing and actuation into wearable form factors. The project bridges ACUTE's vibrotactile research with soft, fabric-embedded electronics &mdash; moving actuator arrays from rigid lab prototypes toward garments suitable for everyday wear.

The work draws on the lab's perceptual findings (actuator placement, frequency sensitivity, sequential vs. simultaneous delivery) to inform the design constraints of textile-integrated haptic systems.
