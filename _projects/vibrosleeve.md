A forearm-worn vibrotactile display with six voice-coil actuators arranged in a 2&times;3 array, designed to deliver Braille-like spatial patterns through the skin. The device serves as the shared hardware platform across several ACUTE projects.

Sequential presentation of patterns achieves **93% recognition accuracy** versus 26% with simultaneous stimulation. At optimal interstimulus intervals (300 ms), short patterns reach 92&ndash;98% accuracy. The system has been validated with 14 distinct patterns and tested for both frequency and location effects on the forearm.
