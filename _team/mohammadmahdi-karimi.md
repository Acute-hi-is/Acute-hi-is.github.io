Mohammadmahdi holds BSc and MSc degrees in Biomedical Engineering. His doctoral research focuses on the design and development of tactile sensory feedback systems to enhance control and perception in bionic prosthetic limbs, conducted in collaboration with Össur and funded by the Rannis Technical Development Fund.
